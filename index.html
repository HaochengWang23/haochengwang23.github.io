Biography

I received my Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics from CUHKSZ in July 2026. During my master's study, I was supervised by Yuan Gao and Tin Lun LAM. I obtained my Bachelor's Degree from Beihang University in 2024. I used to be an intern at Shenzhen AIRS Laboratory in 2025. From April to June 2026, I worked as a research intern at the BigEye Lab, Tsinghua University, under the supervision of Postdoc Sen Cui. Since July 2026, I have been working as a Research Assistant at Shenzhen Loop Area Institute, where I am advised by Prof. Ben Fei from CUHK.

I have long been interested in enabling robots to autonomously explore and understand the physical world, as well as in natural human-robot interaction. This interest has motivated me to explore related areas such as robotics, reinforcement learning, world models, and multi-robot collaboration. During my undergraduate studies, I worked on bio-inspired underwater robotics and spatial perception for autonomous driving. During my master's studies, I focused on heterogeneous multi-robot collaboration. Since 2025, I have become particularly interested in realizing physical intelligence through world models and robotic systems. For my Ph.D., I hope to further pursue this direction during my Ph.D. study.

I am currently seeking Ph.D. opportunities in Embodied AI, world model and related areas.
